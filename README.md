# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis 🛒📊

## 📌 Project Overview
This project features an in-depth transactional behavior analysis of 3,900 purchases to drive data-centric product positioning, customer loyalty, and targeted marketing strategies[cite: 1, 2]. By leveraging a robust data pipeline, this project analyzes customer spending patterns, product preferences, and subscription behaviors to generate actionable business intelligence[cite: 2].

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Python (pandas, SQLAlchemy, psycopg2):** Used for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), missing data imputation, feature engineering, and database loading[cite: 2, 3].
* **PostgreSQL:** Used for structured querying to answer key business questions and aggregate transactional metrics[cite: 2, 5].
* **Power BI:** Used to build an interactive behavioral dashboard for tracking high-volume customers, category revenue, and demographic sales[cite: 1, 2].

## 📂 Dataset Information
The dataset consists of a cleansed transactional registry with structural and relational integrity[cite: 1]. 
* **Size:** 3,900 rows and 18 transactional dimensions[cite: 1, 2].
* **Key Features:** 
  * **Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status[cite: 2].
  * **Purchase Details:** Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color[cite: 2].
  * **Shopping Behavior:** Discount Applied, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Shipping Type[cite: 2].

## 🚀 Methodology

### 1. Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering (Python)
* **Missing Data Resolution:** Imputed 37 missing values in the `Review Rating` column using median indices calculated dynamically across product categories[cite: 1, 2, 3].
* **Standardization:** Converted all feature columns to a uniform `snake_case` format for optimized SQL query mapping[cite: 1, 2, 3].
* **Feature Creation:** Binned customer ages into an `age_group` column and engineered a `purchase_frequency_days` metric mapping textual frequencies to numerical day counts[cite: 2, 3].
* **Redundancy Control:** Validated the overlap between discount and promotional metrics, removing the highly redundant `promo_code_used` column[cite: 1, 2, 3]. 
* **Statistical Formulation:** Standard representation of Mean Transaction Purchase Amount was formulated as $\overline{x}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}x_{i}}{N}$[cite: 1].

### 2. Transactional Analysis (SQL)
A series of optimized queries were executed to extract business value, including:
* Calculating total revenue generated by male vs. female cohorts[cite: 2, 5].
* Identifying high-spending discount users who purchased above the average threshold[cite: 2, 5].
* Classifying customers into "New," "Returning," and "Loyal" segments based on cumulative purchase history[cite: 2, 5].
* Evaluating revenue contributions split by binned age groups[cite: 2, 5].

### 3. Data Visualization (Power BI)
Built a dynamic behavioral dashboard tracking 970 high-volume localized customers, visualizing an average transaction value of $1.248K and highlighting that the Clothing and Accessories segments dominate overall performance[cite: 1, 2].

## 💡 Key Insights
* **Customer Loyalty:** A massive 79.9% of the customer base (3,116 users) is classified as "Loyal," suggesting exceptionally strong retention indicators[cite: 1, 2].
* **Demographic Disparity:** Male buyers significantly outspend females, generating $157,890 in revenue compared to $75,191[cite: 1, 2]. "Young Adults" lead overall revenue generation at $62,143[cite: 1, 2].
* **The Subscription Gap:** Unsubscribed buyers constitute 73% of transactional volume (2,847 customers)[cite: 1, 2]. Notably, 72% of high-value repeat buyers (2,518 users) are currently unsubscribed, representing a massive monetization opportunity[cite: 1, 2].
* **Shipping & Discounts:** Express delivery yields a 3.45% higher average spend than standard shipping[cite: 1]. Products like Hats (50.0%) and Sneakers (49.6%) are highly dependent on discounts to sell[cite: 1, 2].

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Monetize Subscriptions:** Incentivize the 2,518 unsubscribed repeat buyers with tailored exclusive discounts, membership tiers, and customized benefits[cite: 1, 2].
2. **Pricing Optimization:** Reduce promotional exposure on baseline, discount-dependent items (like Hats and Sneakers) to systematically reclaim standard retail profit margins[cite: 1, 2].
3. **Targeted Demographics:** Focus marketing campaigns toward high-value male cohorts and young adults, placing emphasis on premier-rated items like Gloves and Sandals[cite: 1, 2].
